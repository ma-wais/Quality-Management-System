1. Context Analysis Record
Template (Columns): Date | Internal/External Factor | Description | Impact | Action
Usage: Once annually or upon significant change.

2. Interested Parties Record
Template: Party | Needs | Expectations | Follow-up Method
Usage: Annual review.

3. Risks and Opportunities Record
Template: Risk/Opportunity | Impact | Likelihood | Action | Responsible | Review
Usage: Monthly update.

4. Quality Objectives Record
Template: Objective | Indicator | Target Value | Responsible | Review
Usage: Quarterly follow-up.

5. Competence & Training - Job Description Record
Template: Title | Tasks | Qualifications | KPI | Approval Date
Usage: Upon hiring or update.

6. Competence & Training - Employee File
Template: Name | Position | Hire Date | Contract | Notes
Usage: Updated upon any change.

7. Competence & Training - Training Record
Template: Employee | Training | Date | Provider | Evaluation
Usage: After each training.

8. Competence & Training - Performance Evaluation
Template: Employee | Objectives | Evaluation | Improvement | Decision
Usage: Semi-annually/Annually.

9. Supplier Management - Service Delivery Record
Template: Date | Beneficiary | Service | Description | Employee | Duration | Notes
Usage: Daily after each service.

10. Individual Plans Record
Template: Beneficiary | Objective | Plan | Duration | Evaluation
Usage: At service start and periodic review.

11. Complaints Record (Beneficiaries)
Template: Number | Date | Complaint | Action | Responsible | Status
Usage: For each complaint until closure.

12. Beneficiary Satisfaction Record
Template: Period | Tool | Result | Improvement
Usage: Quarterly.

13. Resources Record
Template: Type | Description | Status | Need | Action
Usage: Periodic review.

14. Maintenance Record
Template: Equipment | Date | Maintenance | Responsible | Notes
Usage: Upon each maintenance.

15. Communication Record
Template: Date | Party | Subject | Method | Result
Usage: Upon each formal communication.

16. KPI Record
Template: Indicator | Value | Target | Status | Action
Usage: Monthly.

17. Nonconformity Record
Template: Date | Description | Cause | Action | Closure
Usage: Upon any defect occurrence.

18. CAPA Record
Template: Problem | Corrective Action | Responsible | Review
Usage: After nonconformity.

19. Internal Audit Record
Template: Date | Area | Observations | Action
Usage: Per audit plan.

20. Management Review Record
Template: Date | Topics | Decisions | Follow-up
Usage: Semi-annually/Annually.

21. Document Control Record
Template: Document Name | Code | Version | Date | Status
Usage: Upon any release/modification.

22. Records Control Record
Template: Record Name | Storage Location | Duration | Disposal
Usage: Annual review.
